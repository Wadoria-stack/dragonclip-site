DragonClip

DragonClip is a personal video tool that runs on its owner's own Windows computer. It cuts and edits short videos (clips and game reviews) and can send the finished videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

Terms of Service Privacy Policy

How DragonClip works with TikTok

DragonClip uses the official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.

  1. The owner logs in to TikTok once and allows DragonClip to upload videos.
  2. When a video is finished, DragonClip uploads it to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  3. The owner opens TikTok on their phone, adds a caption and posts the video themselves.

DragonClip never posts anything publicly by itself. It only uploads drafts.

Who can use it

DragonClip is a private tool used by its owner only. It is not offered to the public.
